# Pixora Studio — complete static website

Upload the **contents of this folder** to the root of your GitHub Pages repository. Do not upload the ZIP itself or the outer folder.

## Included pages and files

- `index.html` — homepage
- `about.html`, `services.html`, `work.html`, `contact.html`
- `guides/` — guide index and three original educational articles
- `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `disclaimer.html`, `cookie-policy.html`
- `404.html`, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`
- `style.css`, `main.js`

## Before requesting an AdSense review

1. Confirm that `quiltclient@zohomail.in` is the correct, actively monitored public contact address. Update it in `contact.html`, all policy pages and `main.js` if necessary.
2. Replace every `YOUR-PUBLISHED-DOMAIN` entry in `sitemap.xml` with the exact HTTPS domain used by the live site. Add `Sitemap: https://YOUR-DOMAIN/sitemap.xml` to `robots.txt` after doing so.
3. Review the privacy, cookie, terms and disclaimer pages against your actual business, hosting provider, email handling, audience and any tools you enable. These pages are general templates, not legal advice.
4. Add only genuine, useful original portfolio work. The current work page clearly labels its examples as practice concepts; do not present them as client projects.
5. Make sure every service described is genuinely offered and that enquiries receive a response. Add real pricing, delivery details or business information only when accurate.
6. Test every navigation link, form, policy link and page on mobile and desktop. The contact form opens an email draft; it does not send or store data itself.
7. If you add Google AdSense, analytics, embeds or other third-party scripts, update the policies and implement any required consent controls for the regions you serve. Do not place ads on empty pages, error pages, contact-only states or pages with little publisher content.
8. Verify the site in Google Search Console and AdSense. A technically complete site does not guarantee AdSense approval; Google makes the final decision based on the live site and its policies.

## Contact form

The form uses `mailto:` and depends on the visitor having an email application configured. It does not submit to a server or store form data. Review the draft before sending.
