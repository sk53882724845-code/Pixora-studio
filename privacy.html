Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how Pixora Studio handles information when you visit this website or contact the studio. It applies to information collected through this website. Third-party websites and services have their own privacy practices.

Information you provide

If you contact Pixora Studio by email, the studio receives the information you choose to include, which may include your name, email address and project details. The contact form on this website prepares a message in your device's email application. It does not submit information to a Pixora Studio website server or store form entries in a website database.

How information is used

Information you send may be used to reply to your enquiry, understand a requested project, provide requested services and keep necessary business records. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers or other sensitive information through the contact form.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This website package does not include analytics, advertising tags or intentional advertising cookies. The hosting provider may process basic technical information needed to deliver and secure the website. If analytics, advertising, embedded media or other third-party services are added later, this policy must be updated to identify those services, explain relevant data use and provide any legally required consent or choice mechanisms before they are enabled. Advertising availability and cookie behaviour may vary by region.

Sharing and retention

Pixora Studio does not sell personal information. Information may be handled by email, hosting or other service providers where necessary to operate the website or respond to you, or disclosed when required by law. Enquiry messages should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, project, record-keeping or legal obligations, and then deleted or securely disposed of where appropriate.

Security and children's privacy

Reasonable care should be taken to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. This website is not designed to request personal information from children. If the website's audience or data practices change, appropriate legal and privacy safeguards must be reviewed.

Your choices and contact

You may contact Pixora Studio to ask about personal information you have sent or request correction or deletion where applicable law provides that right. Email: quiltclient@zohomail.in.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website or its data practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
